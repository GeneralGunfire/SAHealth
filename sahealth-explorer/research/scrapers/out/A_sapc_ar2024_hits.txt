IBILITIES The South African Pharmacy Council (hereinafter referred to as “SAPC”/“Council”) is an  independent statutory health professional council established in terms of the Pharmacy  Act, 53 of 1974, to regulate the pharmacy profession, which includes pharmacists,  pharmacy support personnel and pharmacies in both the public and private sector.  The SAPC is vested with statutory powers of peer review and embraces as its objectives  those outlined in the Pharmacy Act.  The SAPC is responsible for its own funding and endorses the principles contained in  the King IV Code on Corporate Governance (2016)
