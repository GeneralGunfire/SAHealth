National Health Act, 2003 (Act No. 61 of 2003)
Chapter 2 : Rights and Duties of Users and Health Care Personnel
14. Confidentiality
Purchase
(1)
All information concerning a 
user
, including information relating to his or her health status, treatment or stay in a 
health establishment
, is confidential.
(2)
Subject to 
section 15
, no person may disclose any information contemplated in unless— 
(a)
the user consents to that disclosure in writing;
(b)
a court order or any law requires that disclosure; or
(c)
non-disclosure of the information represents a serious threat to public health.
